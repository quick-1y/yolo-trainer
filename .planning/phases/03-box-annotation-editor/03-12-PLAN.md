## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): "A user can annotate images one by one with bounding boxes in a fast, keyboard-driven editor without ever losing work". This plan delivers the failure half of "without ever losing work": retries, conflicts, rejected saves, load errors and the orientation guard.

<objective>
Every way a save or a load can fail is visible, recoverable and never silent: transient failures retry with backoff, conflicts stop and offer Reload, rejected saves resync, stale histories are dropped, broken or mis-oriented images block editing, and Ctrl+S saves on demand.

Purpose: D-09, D-11 and D-12 promise autosave without silent loss; Pitfalls 4 and 14 are the two ways the editor could store wrong data without noticing.
Output: saver backoff + error classification, Ctrl+S, `ConflictBanner`, read-only mode, re-entry check, load-error and mismatch states, the 2000-box limit.
</objective>
