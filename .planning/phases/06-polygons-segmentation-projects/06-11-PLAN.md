<objective>
While a segment model trains, the user watches mask metrics as the primary solid lines and box metrics as dashed companions on the same three charts, with `seg` in the loss breakdown and the misleading training-only `sem` term kept out of the sums (D-13).

Purpose: success criterion 4's chart half, built on the keys captured from the real segment run (Plan 06-05), not on guesses.
Output: `secondary_metrics` and `secondary` frames, fold support for box fields, `sem` exclusion, segment chart series and captions, a frontend fixture of the captured run.
</objective>
