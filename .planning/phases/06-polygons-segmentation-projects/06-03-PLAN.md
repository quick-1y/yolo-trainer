ROADMAP goal (verbatim, not in user-story form): "A user can annotate `segment` projects with polygons, drawn by hand or generated with one click, and train segmentation models end-to-end". No story is invented; run `/gsd-mvp-phase 6` if one is wanted.

<objective>
A user selects a polygon and reshapes it the Label Studio / CVAT way (D-02): drags vertices, adds a vertex from a faded midpoint handle and moves the whole polygon — each as one undoable gesture and one save.

Purpose: the "adding and moving vertices" half of success criterion 1 ("edits it by adding, moving, and deleting vertices, and assigns a class"; deleting and class assignment are Plan 06-22), and it makes click-to-segment results reshapable like hand-drawn shapes (success criterion 2, Plan 06-08).
Output: interactive `PolygonShape` (select, hover, vertex, midpoint and body drag), vertex helpers, `updatePolygon`, selection wiring.
</objective>
