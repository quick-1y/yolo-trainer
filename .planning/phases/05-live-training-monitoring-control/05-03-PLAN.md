## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim, not in user-story form): "A user can watch training progress live, stop or resume runs, understand failures, and browse the history of all runs". No user story is invented (run `/gsd-mvp-phase 5` for one).

<objective>
On a slow CPU epoch the page never looks frozen: the user sees the batch progress inside the current epoch and a whole-run ETA, and at the end the final validation is shown as "Final validation…" followed by a "Final results (best.pt)" card instead of a fake extra epoch.

Purpose: D-03 and D-04 (in-epoch progress, ETA, final validation labeling) and the progress event schema v2 the rest of the phase relies on (D-05: train_start segment marker, last_epoch, fitness).
Output: train_job `make_callbacks` emitting v2 events (+ unit tests with a fake trainer and clock), fake trainer v2 events, fold batch/segment/phase state, `eta.ts`, `BatchProgress`, `FinalResultsCard`, RunPage running/completed cards, i18n.
</objective>
