## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim, not in user-story form): "A user can watch training progress live, stop or resume runs, understand failures, and browse the history of all runs". No user story is invented (run `/gsd-mvp-phase 5` for one).

<objective>
A user sees all three training charts (loss, mAP, precision/recall) live on a running run and rebuilt for every past run, with exact values in a tooltip, the best epoch and resume boundaries marked, and clear states while the stream loads, reconnects or is unavailable.

Purpose: completes D-01, D-02 and D-05 on top of the Plan 05-01 tracer (frontend only; the stream already sends every event raw and enriched).
Output: full `foldProgress` with tests, `ChartTooltip`, complete `RunCharts`, stream connection states in `useRunStream`, `runPollInterval`, i18n.
</objective>
